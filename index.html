About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at VIC Lab, KAIST, advised by Prof. Munchurl Kim. I am expecting to receive my Ph.D. in August 2026.

My research focuses on computer vision, especially image and video restoration (video super-resolution, deblurring, and diffusion-based restoration), with an emphasis on the real-world application perspective.
